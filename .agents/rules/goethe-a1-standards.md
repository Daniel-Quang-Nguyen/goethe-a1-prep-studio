# Goethe A1 Content Standards

1. **Preposition Matrix**:
   - Accusative: *durch, für, gegen, ohne, um, bis*.
   - Dative: *aus, bei, mit, nach, seit, von, zu*.
   - Two-Way (Wechselpräpositionen): *an, auf, hinter, in, neben, über, unter, vor, zwischen*.
     - Location (Wo?): Dative.
     - Direction (Wohin?): Accusative.

2. **Sentence Structure (V2 Rule)**:
   - Declarative: Conjugated verb ALWAYS in Position 2.
   - Yes/No Questions (*Entscheidungsfragen*): Conjugated verb in Position 1.
   - W-Questions (*W-Fragen*): Question word in Position 1, conjugated verb in Position 2.
   - Modal Verbs: Modal in Position 2, infinitive at sentence end (Satzklammer).
   - Separable Verbs: Conjugated verb stem in Position 2, separable prefix at sentence end.
